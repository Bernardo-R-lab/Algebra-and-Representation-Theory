01 — About

Turning symmetry into linear algebra

Representation theory studies how abstract groups act as linear transformations of vector spaces, translating group-theoretic questions into the familiar language of matrices and linear algebra. It is one of the most active areas of modern mathematics, with a strongly interdisciplinary character.

Our group brings together a critical mass of researchers who share a common foundation: deductive reasoning applied to a strongly axiomatic setting. The Department of Mathematics and Applied Mathematics at the University of Pretoria hosts one of the strongest cohorts in algebra and representation theory in South Africa, and several of our themes are pursued nowhere else on the continent. Our work sits at the meeting point of algebra, combinatorics, computational group theory and algebraic coding theory, and moves freely between them. While much of our work centres on finite groups and their representations, the group also pursues combinatorial and geometric group theory, where the objects of study are typically infinite.

Physics

Symmetry governs quantum systems, from the hydrogen atom to the harmonic oscillator.

Chemistry

Representations decompose the vibrations of symmetric molecules into normal modes.

Coding theory

Ideals in group algebras yield error-correcting codes for reliable data transmission.

Cryptography

Group representations underpin robust and homomorphic secret-sharing schemes.

02 — Research

Themes we work in

Six connected subdisciplines, open to neighbouring areas whose questions overlap with ours.

Finite Group Theory

Structure, products and characterisations of finite groups, including questions on Sylow numbers and vanishing elements.

Character Theory of Finite Groups

Character degrees, codegrees and vanishing sets as invariants that constrain the structure of a group.

Representation Theory of Finite Groups

Ordinary and modular representations, characters of Chevalley groups, and their links to finite geometry.

Algebraic Coding Theory

Error-correcting, self-dual and LCD codes built from group algebras, permutation groups and designs.

Combinatorial Design Theory

Designs from maximal subgroups and conjugacy classes, orbit matrices and tactical decompositions.

Non-associative Algebras

Axial algebras of Jordan and Monster type, Matsuo algebras and their flip subalgebras.

Combinatorial & Geometric Group Theory

Cyclically presented groups, hyperbolic groups and free products — combinatorial and geometric methods for infinite groups.
